## About

**Skylit Community** is a community-run home for members of the Skylit Agentic Trading group. It is where we share what we build, compare approaches, and help each other ship.

> [!NOTE]
> This organization is run by community members. It is **not** an official Skylit.ai account, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Skylit, Inc.

## What we build

- **Agents** that read options-flow and dealer-positioning data and act on it
- **APIs and wrappers** around the Skylit REST API
- **MCP servers and tools** that connect Skylit data to AI assistants
- **Templates, notes, and shared utilities** that make the next project faster

Projects may use Heatseeker, Flowseeker, Tempest, or any combination of them. Nothing here is financial advice.

## Getting access

Repositories in this organization are private and open to verified Skylit members only.

1. Open the **Request access** link above.
2. Sign in with Discord so we can confirm your Skylit role.
3. Sign in with GitHub, and you will be invited to the organization automatically.

Access follows your Skylit membership. If your role is removed or you leave the Skylit Discord, your access is removed as well.

## Contributing

1. Read the `README.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository you want to work on.
2. Put your work in your own folder, and keep other members' folders as they are.
3. Never commit API keys, `.env` files, or private data.
4. Be kind and credit other people's work.

## Security

Repositories here are private, so please keep what you see here inside the community. If you find a leaked key or a security problem, tell an organization owner right away.
